A 100% Silicone sealant that provides long lasting adhesion, flexibility, and waterproofing throughout its service life. For bathroom use, around bathtubs and washbins. Can seal joints between glazing, window frames, and door frames.

Available Color/s:

Product Description

SupremPro Multipurpose 100% Silicone Sealant provides a durable, flexible and waterproof seal for the life of the product. Apply between glass and windows and door frames. Apply around the shower area, bathtub and basin. It provides excellent long-term resistance and protection against sun and rain without cracking and shrinking.

  • Seals Joints
  • 100% Waterproof
  • Non shrinkage
  • Anti-fungal

UV and rain resistant.
Non-cracking.

Directions for use
  • Surface must be dry, clean and free from dust and grease.
  • Apply evenly to joint of substrate.
  • Smooth the sealant surface with smoothing tool.
Tips

For joint with depths of over 10 mm, use backing rod to prevent sealant slip into downside of joint.

Setting Time

4-5 mm (depth) / day

Coverage / Net Content

3m for joint dimension 5x5mm
