Product Description

SupremPro Stop Leaks Crystal Clear Waterproof Coating is a high-tech hybrid product (water-based hybrid acrylic emulsion) that prevents water seepage while leaving surfaces 'looking new' again with a Crystal Clear, glossy look once dried. Perfect for repairing hairline & small cracks on walls, floors, decks, terraces and balconies. Creating a 100% Waterproof Stop & a high strength Coating (2-IN-1). A durable, all weather and UV resistant coating. Suitable for both indoor and outdoor applications to ceremic tiles, cement, brick, wood, glass, acrylic sheet, polycarbonate, sandstone, fiber cement & similar porous materials.

  • 100% waterproofing & coating (2-in-1)
  • All weather & UV resistant
  • Crystal clear, glossy finish

Ideal for indoor and outdoor applications on ceramic tile, cement, brick, wood, glass, acrylic, polycarbonate, sandstone, fiber cement and similar porous materials.

Directions for use
  • Surface must be dry, not damp, clean and free from dust and grease.
  • Apply Crystal Clear Waterproof Coating with a brush with minimum 2 layers.
  • Each layer should cross the other for best results. Allow each layer to dry for approx 30-45 mins before applying next layer.
Tips
  1. Avoid using a roller as it may reduce the strength of the coating.
  2. Clean surface with a soft material to protect coated surface.
  3. Avoid using abrasive cleaning products.
  4. It is overpaintable.
  5. Dispense only the amount of product you need from the packaging to prevent contamination from the brush.
  6. Not recommended for bathroom or humid area
Setting Time

Full cure 24 - 48 hours

Coverage

2 - 5 sqm/kg ( 2 layers) subject to the porousness of the materials
