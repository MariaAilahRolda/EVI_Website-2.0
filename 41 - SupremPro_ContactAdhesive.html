Product Description

SupremPro Repair Express Contact Adhesive provides strong adhesion and a flexible bond for shoe repairs and bonding materials such as wood, decorative laminate, rubber, leather, cork, formica, fabric and plywood. The adhesive is safe and Toluene Free (Toluene is a substance known to be harmful to the nervous and respiratory systems).

  • Safe to use
  • Strong adhesion
  • Flexible
  • Perfect for household

The adhesive is safe & Toluene free (Toluene is a substance known to be harmful to the nervous and respiratory systems).

Directions for use
  • Surface must be dry, clean and free from dust and grease.
  • Apply adhesive to both surfaces and wait 10-15 mins, or until the adhesive is no longer tacky.
  • Carefully join the surfaces, pressing firmly from the centre outwards to prevent air bubbles and provide maximum adhesion.
Tips
  1. The product is overpaintable.
  2. Can be applied to wet / moist surafces.
  3. For joints with depths of over 10 mm., use backing rod to prevent sealant slip into downside of joint.
