Product Description

SupremPro Stop Leaks Butyl Waterproof Tape prevents and fixes leaks for all kinds of roofs, gutters, joints between walls and floors, drainage pipes, exhaust ducts, roof flashing and roof turbines. Works well with expansion joints and on most materials such as metals, cement, fibre cement, uPVC, wood, brick and many more. A self-adhesive sealing tape that is coated with aluminum foil on its upper side.

  • For roof, gutter & flashing
  • Seals and stops leaks
  • 100% waterproof
  • Long durability

Ideal for all types of materials including metal, cement, uPVC, fiber cement, wood, brick and many more. A self-adhesive sealing tape that is coated with aluminum foil on its upper side. For long-lasting durability, it is recommended to apply a topcoat of paint or acrylic waterproofing.

Directions for use
  • Surface must be dry, clean and free from dust and grease.
  • Cut tape, remove film, and apply firmly. Reposition within 5 mins if needed.
  • Overlap ends by at least 5 cm.
  • For outdoor use: overpaint with paint or acrylic waterproof for added durability.
  • After 24 hours, the glue will work perfectly.
Setting Time

Full cure 24 hours
