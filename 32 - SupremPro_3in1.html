Product Description

SupremPro 3-IN-1 Polymer Sealant is a superior adhesive that suits most common indoor and outdoor sealing, bonding and waterproofing works. Provides a strong bond, without primer, and quick grab. Perfect for high strength sealing and bonding in construction, decorative panel for wall and applications that need flexible adhesion. Bonds porous and non-porous substrates such as PVC, ABS, polycarbonate, WPC wall panel, stone veneer, stainless, aluminum, steel, copper, wood, plywood, fiber cement board, concrete, mortar, natural stone, tile, glass, porcelain etc.

  • High adhesion sealant & waterproof
  • Fast curing, firm bond, compatible with all materials
  • Works on wet & moist surfaces
  • All weather resistant

Ideal for porous and non porous surfaces including PVC, ABS, polycarbonate, WPC boards, vinyl, stainless steel, aluminum, iron, copper, wood, engineered wood, fiber cement board, concrete, plaster, natural stone, glass, tile, porcelain and more.

Directions for use
  • Surface must be dry, clean and free from dust and grease.
  • Cut nozzle at 45-degree angle to the desired bead size.
  • Apply evenly to substrate using a cartridge gun.
  • Smooth the sealant surface with smoothing tool.
Tips
  1. Suitable for underwater applications or submerged areas for bonding purposes only (not recommended for sealing).
  2. Avoid use on glass subjected to prolonged heat exposure.
  3. The product is overpaintable.
Setting Time

Approx 24 hours
