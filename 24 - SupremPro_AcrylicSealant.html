Product Description

SupremPro Multipurpose Acrylic Sealant is perfect for sealing joints between concrete and indoor windows and door frames; also joints and gaps between skirting boards and timber floors and stairs. Finishes woodworking tasks like built-in furniture installation. Works well on porous materials, concrete, mortar, timber, gypsum, stone, aluminium and uPVC. Provides good elasticity and a durable seal. Easy to match timber shades. Over paintable.

  • Fills & seals gaps
  • Indoor use
  • Supports expansion joints

Ideal for porous materials such as concrete, plaster, wood, gypsum and natural stone, as well as aluminium and uPVC. Provides excellent flexibility and is easy to color match for a seamless wood finish.

Directions for use
  • Surface must be dry, clean and free from dust and grease.
  • Cut nozzle at 45-degree angle to the desired bead size.
  • Apply to substrate with the cartridge gun.
Tips

For joints with depths of over 10 mm., use backing rod to prevent sealant slip into downside of joint.

Setting Time

curing rate 1 mm (depth) / 24 hours

Coverage

12 m for joint dimension 5x5mm
