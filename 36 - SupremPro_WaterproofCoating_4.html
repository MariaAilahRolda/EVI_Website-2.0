Product Description

SupremPro Stop Leaks Waterproof Coating & Roof Sealer is an acrylic polymer for sealing Roofs, Decks and Walls. This flexible product prevents and repairs cracks (<=5 mm) and leaks, including flowing into small hairline cracks. Delivering a jointless seal that is 100% Waterproof and UV resistant. It is a long-lasting product providing 10 years durability*. Adheres well on concrete, cement, mortar, monier tiles, wood, galvanized steel and aluminium surfaces. This water-based product is highly elastic, with a very low odour, making it easy to apply with smooth adhesion. Has anti-fungal and anti-algae properties. Thick adhesive content. No fibre mesh needed.

  • Suitable for roofs, decks & walls
  • 100% waterproof & seal 10 years protection
  • Has anti-fungal & anti-algae properties

Ideal for adhesion to concrete, cement, plaster, roof tiles, wood, galvanized steel and aluminum. Low odor, smooth finish, and easy to apply. The adhesive content. No fibre mesh needed.

Directions for use
  • Surface must be clean (please spray with water), free from dust & grease and dry before apply the product.
  • Applying by brush, roller, or spatula, in at least 3 layers for long durability. Layers should cross the other at right angles.
  • Let it dry completely for 24-48 hours.
Setting Time:

Approx. 30-45 mins per layer, depending on conditions, and cured in 24-48 hours.

Coverage

1 kg/m² (at 1 mm thickness)
